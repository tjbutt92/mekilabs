How to play

You have 2 movement each turn. Clear hexes cost 1, forest costs 2, a path is free, and mountains block. Reach your checkpoints in order, then the finish.

Before you move, use MAP to lay a shape. Mountains and forests go on clear ground. Paths can cover clear ground or forest. You cannot wall off either racer. After you move, building waits until next turn.
