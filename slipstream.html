How to play

Pick at least two teams. Each team places two riders, one at a time, when the banner shows that colour. The rider furthest ahead moves first. When riders are level, the red lane goes before black, and black before blue. That order continues back through the field. Tap a lit space to move forward or change lane.

Exert spends 1 energy to move twice. Rest gains 1 energy, up to 3. A teammate directly behind is pulled into the space you leave. With All Slipstream on, everyone in that line is pulled too, until the first gap. The draft is free and does not replace their turn. After lap 1, a rider left a full lap down is out. The last rider's team wins.
