Caddie's Notes

💨 Trajectory

Low (Line Drive): Punches through wind easily, but takes a massive bounce and rolls far.
High (Floater): Gets pushed heavily by the wind, but lands soft with very little roll.

↩️ Shot Shaping

Curving the ball (Left/Right) fights crosswinds and helps wrap around hazards. However, curving the ball causes it to lose forward distance in the air! It will land short and wide, then take a hard bounce back inward.

Wind, slopes, and scoring

The wind range is how many hexes the ball can be pushed after you pick a landing spot. Arrows are slopes: rolling with one is cheap, rolling against one costs extra, and a ball that stops on a slope keeps sliding that way.
One shot per hole. Lowest score wins. Score is hexes from the pin, plus a penalty: fairway 1, rough 2, bunker 3, water or out of bounds 4. The hole itself is 0.
