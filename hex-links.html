Caddie's Notes

💨 Trajectory

Low (Line Drive): Subtracts 2 from the wind, and the push never goes below 0. A 2–3 wind becomes 0–1. It then takes a massive bounce and rolls far. While Low is selected, "Wind: -2" appears beside Bnc and Roll.
High (Floater): Takes the full wind on the badge, but lands soft with very little roll.

↩️ Shot Shaping

Curving the ball (Left/Right) fights crosswinds and helps wrap around hazards. However, curving the ball causes it to lose forward distance in the air! It will land short and wide, then take a hard bounce back inward.

Wind, slopes, and scoring

The wind range is how many hexes the ball can be pushed after you pick a landing spot. That is the wind box at the top left. A low shot takes 2 off that range, never going below 0, and shows "Wind: -2" beside Bnc and Roll. Landing Hint (the ◎ button, or the switch on the start screen) marks only the hex the shot comes down on before wind, bounce, and roll. A straight shot uses the hex you picked. A curve uses one fixed hex to that side. Arrows are slopes: rolling with one is cheap, rolling against one costs extra, and a ball that stops on a slope keeps sliding that way.
One shot per hole. Lowest score wins. Score is hexes from the pin, plus a penalty: fairway 1, rough 2, bunker 3, water or out of bounds 4. The hole itself is 0.
