TACTICAL AREA CONTROL

Preview

Match Rules

First team to score 3 goals wins.

How to play

Each side places two tokens in their own half. After kickoff, place a token to grow your control, then pass the ball. Squares touching more of your tokens than your opponent's are yours. A tie is contested, and a pass can enter it but not travel through it. Finish in the far goal. Click the tick, or press Enter, to confirm.
